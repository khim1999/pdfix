Privacy Policy for PDFix

Last Updated: October 20, 2026

Summary (The Short Version):
  • 100% On-Device Processing: All PDF operations run locally on your device hardware.
  • Zero Cloud Uploads: Your documents are never uploaded to any remote server or cloud.
  • Zero Data Collection: No names, emails, telemetry, or analytics trackers.
  • Privacy Protection: Includes 4-Digit PIN Lock, Anti-Spy (FLAG_SECURE), and Storage Cache Shredder.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Unlike cloud PDF converters, PDFix functions completely offline. When you view, compress, merge, split, or convert a PDF, all processing is performed on your device CPU and memory. We do not require account registration, and we do not collect personal identities, advertising IDs, or geolocation.

2. Device Permissions

PDFix requests only the permissions strictly required to perform local file operations:

  • Storage / Media (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Required solely to read PDF documents you select and to save exported files.
  • Network (INTERNET): Present for system network connectivity verification; no document contents or telemetry are transmitted.
  • Package Queries (<queries>): Used on Android 11+ to verify whether official client apps (such as Telegram, Microsoft Teams, Microsoft Outlook, or WhatsApp) are installed when you tap "Direct Share".

3. Document Sharing

PDFix never shares documents without your explicit action. When you tap "Share", the file is passed directly through Android's standard FileProvider to the target app chosen by you.

4. Built-in Security

PDFix features on-device security tools:

  • App Lock (PIN): Protected with salted SHA-256 cryptographic hashing stored in local sandbox storage.
  • Anti-Spy Screen (FLAG_SECURE): Prevents screenshots and masks recent app multitask previews.
  • Storage Shredder: Permanently purges orphaned temporary cache files on demand.

5. Third-Party Services & Children's Privacy

PDFix contains no third-party analytics SDKs, trackers, or advertising networks. We do not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this policy, contact TTFC at: support@ttfc-pdfix.local
